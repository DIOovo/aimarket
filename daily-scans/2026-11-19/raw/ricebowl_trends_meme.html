Start with the effect, then the scene

Effect templates such as aura and camera orbit work with almost any photo. Scene and prop templates need a clear, well-lit shot of your whole body so the prop lines up with your pose.

How to make a clip in three steps

  1. 1. Pick a template from the list above and open its page.
  2. 2. Upload the photo the template asks for. Use a clear, front-facing shot with even light. Couple and group templates need one clear photo per person.
  3. 3. Wait for the render, check the clip, then download it. Keep the audio on when you post, because each clip is built around its original sound.

Each clip is rendered from your photo and the template's reference, so the result depends on the photo you upload. If the first render misses, try a different photo angle before you switch templates. Every template is free to try.

Photo tips for better results

  • Use a sharp photo with your face in the upper third of the frame.
  • Even daylight or soft indoor light gives a cleaner result than a harsh flash.
  • A plain background keeps the edges clean when a template swaps the scene.
  • Take off sunglasses and keep hands away from the face unless the template calls for them.

AI Meme Trends: questions

How do AI meme trends work?

Upload a photo, choose a meme template and the tool applies the format to your face or your whole scene. The result is a short clip you can download.

How long are the clips?

Clips follow the length of the reference video. Only references longer than 15 seconds are cut down to 15 seconds.

Do I need a full-body photo?

Only for scene and prop templates. Effect templates work with a head-and-shoulders photo.

Can I combine two memes?

Make two separate clips and join them in your editor, since each template renders one scene.

Which of these AI meme trends gets the most reactions?

The zoom-out and clone formats are the most recognizable, so they land fastest with a friend group.
